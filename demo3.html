Help & information

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a real donation through this demo?

No. The payment and goods donation flows are simulations. No money or real donation request is sent.

Are the listed care homes real?

No. All names, locations, and needs shown here are fictional examples.

Why are there no public child profiles?

Children’s privacy and safety matter. A real platform should avoid publishing identifying details or images without appropriate consent and safeguards.
